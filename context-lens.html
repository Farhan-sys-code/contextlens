How it works

1EmbedTitle + summary become a 1024-d vector with BAAI/bge-large.
2ReduceUMAP compresses to 5 dimensions, keeping neighbours close.
3ClusterHDBSCAN finds natural topic groups, no preset count.
4LabelEach cluster centroid is matched to a category by cosine similarity.
5ClassifyNew articles are scored against the categories instantly.
